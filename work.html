Selected Work.
Made With Purpose.

A collection of ideas brought to life through identity, editorial design, photography, and visual storytelling.

01 / Brand Identity

BAM

A brand identity developed for the Bits, Atoms, Molecules (BAM) Innovation Lab. I created the visual system, exploring geometric forms, typography, and color to connect digital design with hands-on fabrication.

02 / Editorial Design

Talon Magazine

Editorial design for Talon Magazine, combining bold typography, photography, and intentional layouts to bring student stories to life. I designed the cover and feature spreads, balancing visual impact with readability and a consistent editorial identity.

03 / Brand Development

FAMH

An independent clothing and lifestyle brand built around identity, connection, and chosen family. I developed the brand identity, garment concepts, and creative direction, combining heritage-inspired design with contemporary streetwear to tell a story of belonging beyond blood.

04 / Photography

Through My Lens

A collection of photography exploring people, places, movement, and everyday moments. Through composition, lighting, and color, I focus on capturing atmosphere and telling stories that go beyond the image itself.

05 / Video & Visual Storytelling

Euro2Morocco

A travel film exploring movement, place, and visual storytelling.
